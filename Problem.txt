32. Strong future upgrade: automatic ingestion

Possible sources:

Google Calendar
University LMS

Shared calendars

Then Pikul automatically understands existing commitments.

Instead of:

User manually enters 10 tasks

the final demo could say:

“Pikul already knows I have an assignment Friday, a presentation Thursday, two shifts and a club event because it pulled the commitments from tools I already use.”

This directly addresses the adoption concern.

33. Good use of AI

We should not add AI merely as a chatbot.

Weak:

User:
"I'm stressed."

AI:
"Remember to rest!"

That adds little differentiation.

AI would be more useful for understanding unstructured commitments.

Example incoming email:

“Our demo moved to Thursday. Please finish the slides by Wednesday night.”

AI could extract:

Commitment:
Prepare presentation slides

Deadline:
Wednesday evening

Related event:
Demo Thursday

Category:
Coursework

Estimated workload:
Medium

The deterministic workload engine can then evaluate the extracted commitment.

This combines AI where language understanding is useful with deterministic maths where consistency is important.

Receive/understand a request
↓
Read existing schedule
↓
Calculate impact
↓
Search possible alternatives
↓
Recommend an action
↓
Generate response
↓
With explicit permission, perform action

Example:

"Can you cover Saturday's shift?"

↓

Pikul detects:
Sunday assignment deadline

↓

Full shift:
too expensive

↓

Alternative:
4pm–7pm only

↓

Suggested reply:
"I can't cover the whole shift,
but I can take 4–7pm if that helps."

The important distinction is:

AI should reason across the actual schedule and help negotiate commitments, not simply chat.
